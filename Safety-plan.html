How to help me

A simple guide for Mum and my sisters when I am becoming unwell or overwhelmed.

Start here

  1. Stay calm and speak slowly.
  2. Stay nearby if it is safe. Keep the room quiet and reduce pressure.
  3. Ask one short question: “What would help right now?”
  4. If I cannot answer, suggest one familiar calming thing from the list below.

Warning signs

Please check in with me if you notice one or more of these changes.

  • My thoughts seem to be steering me towards things I would not normally do, or they seem to take over.
  • I mention suicidal thoughts, not wanting to be here, or being unable to keep myself safe.
  • I seem frightened, watched, unsafe, threatened, or unusually suspicious.
  • I am sleeping very little, or my insomnia is becoming much worse.
  • I am seeing things that do not change back, including figures or entities.
  • Sounds or voices feel aggressive, threatening, or aimed at harming me.

How to respond

The distress is real even when you do not share what I am experiencing.

Something helpful to say “I can see this is frightening. I am here with you. We do not have to work it all out right now.”

Do

  • Use plain language and short sentences.
  • Give me time to answer.
  • Acknowledge how frightened or distressed I feel.
  • Keep things quiet, familiar, and low-pressure.
  • Ask before touching me or moving things.

Please avoid

  • Arguing about what I see, hear, or believe.
  • Agreeing that frightening conclusions are definitely true.
  • Guessing or filling in missing details.
  • Asking many questions at once.
  • Taking confused or unusual speech personally.

Things that may help

Suggest one thing gently. Do not give me the whole list at once.

  • Put familiar music on, or offer my headphones.
  • Get Piff, my weighted dragon plush, and place him near me.
  • Put on my amber light orb.
  • Open my Self Soothe Box and go through it slowly with me.
  • Suggest my game, Piececool model kit, or sticker books.
  • Suggest a familiar game or something familiar to read.
  • Suggest lifting weights only if I feel safe and able. A shorter session is fine.

If it becomes urgent or unsafe

  1. If there is immediate danger, call 999. Do not put yourself in danger.
  2. For urgent mental-health help that is not an immediate emergency, call NHS 111 and select the mental-health option.
  3. Samaritans can be called with me for emotional support on 116 123.
  4. For text support, text SHOUT to 85258.
