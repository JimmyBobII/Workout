SAFETY PLAN

Warning signs — check in if you notice

  • My thoughts seem to be steering me toward things I wouldn't normally do, skipping or taking over.
  • I mention suicidal ideation or not wanting to be here.
  • I seem paranoid — feeling watched, unsafe or threatened.
  • I'm not sleeping or insomnia is getting much worse.
  • I'm seeing things that won't change back (visual hallucinations), or seeing figures/entities.
  • Sounds or voices feel aggressive and feel aimed at harming me (audio hallucinations).

What helps — things to suggest

  • Suggwst I put music on, or suggest I put headphones on.
  • Suggest I get Piff (my weighted dragon plush) — holding him or just having him nearby helps.
  • Suggest I work on my game, Piececool model kit, or sticker books.
  • Suggest a gaming session.
  • Suggest I put on my amber light orb.
  • Suggest reading something familiar.
  • Suggest I lift weights for an hour.
  • Suggest I open my Self Soothe Box and look through everything in it.

If it becomes a crisis

When I'm struggling I may seem irrational or say things that don't make sense — this is the condition, not me being difficult. Don't try to fill in the blanks about what's happening, it can make things worse.

  • Stay calm and stay with me. Just ask me gently what I need. If I can't answer, just stay close and keep things calm and quiet.
  • Don't argue with what I'm seeing or hearing — just be kind and grounded.
  • Samaritans: 116 123 (free, 24/7) — you can call together with me.
  • Crisis text: text SHOUT to 85258.
  • 999 if there is immediate risk.
