My calm and safety plan

Start with one small thing.

I do not need to solve everything. I only need to follow the next step.

You have been here before. You got through it. You can get through this moment.

Do these first

Only focus on these three steps. Tick each one when it is done.

Choose one gentle activity

One option is enough. A shorter version still counts.

  • Open your Self Soothe Box and go through it slowly.
  • Lift weights if it feels safe. A short session is enough if an hour feels too much.
  • Work on your game, Piececool kit, or sticker books.
  • Put on a familiar game.
  • Read something familiar.
When thoughts feel frightening or completely certain

The feeling is real, but I do not have to act on it.

My mind may not be giving me reliable conclusions while I am unwell or overwhelmed.

I can pause important decisions and check what is happening with someone I trust.

Contact someone I trust
Hellena Send a simple message: “Things are not good and I need some company or reassurance.”
Vanda Tell her what is happening and show her this plan.

Remember
